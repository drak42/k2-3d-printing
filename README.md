# K2 3D Printing

Notes, settings and guides for 3D printing on the Creality K2 (K2 Plus, K2 Pro, K2), sliced with
Creality Print and OrcaSlicer.

- [Print Settings for Quality](docs/print-settings.md) — layer lines, smooth surfaces, seams,
  temperatures, speeds, cooling and retraction.
- [Calibration: What Works and What Doesn't](docs/calibration.md) — printer and per-filament
  calibration in order, built-in auto routines versus manual tests.
- [Ceramic Hotends and Silicon Carbide Nozzles](docs/hotend-and-nozzles.md) — what changes with a
  ceramic-heater hotend or SiC nozzle, and the settings and recalibration they need.
- [Suggested Modifications](docs/modifications.md) — no-root, root and major upgrades for the K2
  Plus, the issues they solve, risks of rooting, and what to recalibrate afterwards.
